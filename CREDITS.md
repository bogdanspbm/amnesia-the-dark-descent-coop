## Partner model (`coop_data/partner/`)

The co-op partner's body, hair, skin/eye textures and animations are built from the free **CC0** packs by
**Quaternius** (https://quaternius.com):

- Universal Base Characters
- Universal Animation Library
- Universal Animation Library 2

The clothing layers, cloth/leather textures and normal maps, the lantern mesh and the downed pose are generated by
the scripts in `tools/partner_build/` (Blender). The finished model in `coop_data/partner/` is released under CC0 as
well.

## Game content

No content of the original game (maps, models, textures, sounds, music, text, fonts) is included in this repository
or in its releases. You need your own copy of **Amnesia: The Dark Descent** (Steam or GOG) to play.
The screenshots in `docs/screenshots/` were taken in the running game with the mod.
